About me

Beyond the pattern: production engineering.

I am a pattern maker and technical manager at a Tuscan luxury leather goods manufacturer, where I have handled sampling and industrialisation since 2017. For this work I built Leonardo Pattern Design from scratch: the CAD software that now has over 350 commands for leather goods.

My speciality is turning a bag design into a production-ready model: technical pattern, optimised nesting, material cost analysis and industrialisation.

  • ZWCAD / AutoCAD
  • Bag pattern making
  • Nesting and CAM cutting
  • Cost analysis and BOM
  • Industrialisation
  • VB.NET / AutoLISP / Python
