The standard

Bag difficulty classes

The class depends on structure, number of pieces, interior and hardware. I assign it with the quote, after seeing the 3/4 views and the measurements. Each class has a fixed price per model.

A

Essential

Flat bag without gussets, simple lining, webbing handles. Up to about 10 pieces.

Examples: flat shopper, tote without base
B

Simple

Base or gussets in a single band, one inside pocket, simple closure. Up to about 20 pieces.

Examples: tote with base, simple bucket bag
C

Intermediate

Separate bands and gussets, flap, zip, 2-3 inside pockets, shoulder strap and standard hardware. About 20-35 pieces.

Examples: flap crossbody, shoulder bag
D

Structured

Reinforcements and padding, binding or piping, more compartments, decorative work such as lacing. About 35-50 pieces.

Examples: structured handbag, satchel
E

Complex

Bellows gussets, dividers, several zips and outside pockets, custom hardware, piping. About 50-70 pieces.

Examples: bowling bag, accordion bag
F

Advanced

Many applied components: straps and buckles, quilting, crafted outside pockets, feet, detachable straps. About 70-90 pieces.

Examples: saddle bag with straps, quilted dome bag
G

Superior

Rigid or semi-rigid structures, metal closures and frames built into the pattern, bases with feet, high-end finishing. About 90-110 pieces.

Examples: rigid handbag, structured top handle
H

High complexity

3D shapes and complex developments, joins between different forms, new construction studied from scratch and shape trials. Over 110 pieces.

Examples: rigid box bag, minaudière, sculptural shapes
