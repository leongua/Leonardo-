The tech pack I need

With a photo of the front only, I can give just an estimate. For an exact price and pattern I need the technical drawing with views and measurements, like the examples on this page.

  • Views

    Front, back, side, top and bottom, plus a 3/4 view.

  • Overall dimensions

    Width, height and depth, at the top and at the base if the bag widens or narrows.

  • Style measurements

    Flap and overlap, position of closures, distance between attachments, width of bands, loops and appliqués.

  • Handles and straps

    Drop, width and length; whether they are adjustable or detachable.

  • Interior

    Lining, pockets with their measurements, zips and dividers.

  • Materials and hardware

    Material and colour of each piece; code, size and finish of rings, buckles, closures and feet, or their data sheets.

  • Workmanship

    Painted or turned edges, piping, topstitching, quilting, lacing, logo and tolerances.
