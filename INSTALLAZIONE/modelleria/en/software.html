Leonardo Pattern Design

The leather goods pattern making software I developed. It installs on ZWCAD (2024-2027) and AutoCAD (2018 and 2024-2027) and adds over 350 commands.

Shapes and developments

Panels, bands, gussets, bases, bellows, linings; flattening of 3D surfaces.

Notches and outlines

Notches in several shapes and checks for open or double outlines.

Nesting

Layout of pieces on the hide, with strategies to compare.

Consumption and BOM

Consumption and cost calculation, export to Excel, CSV and HTML.

Cutting

Files for ATOM, AAMA and Gerber machines and plotters.

AI pattern maker

An assistant built into the CAD that reads the drawing and asks for confirmation before changing it.
