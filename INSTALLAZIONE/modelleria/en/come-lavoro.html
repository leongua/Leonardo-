How I work

1

You send me the material

Tech pack with views and measurements, the interior you want and hardware data sheets, if you already have them.

2

Class and quote

I assign a class from A to H to each model and send you the quote with the delivery date.

3

Start with 50%

Work starts with the first payment. Delivery in one week: the Monday after receiving the complete material.

4

Review and balance

You review the work, then pay the remaining 50%.

Payment and invoicing

Two online payments per item through Lemon Squeezy, a platform with international invoicing: suitable for companies based abroad too. You receive an invoice for each payment.

Reading and printing files

No software to buy. DWG/DXF and PDF files open in any CAD viewer and print at 1:1 scale. I guide you through the first opening and printing.

Changes and variants

Agreed beforehand; the surcharges for small additions and variants are in the price list.

Design

If design is needed besides pattern making, it is billed hourly or agreed for the whole collection.

Send me your first model: I’ll reply with class, price and delivery date.
